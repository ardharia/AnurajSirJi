# Step 19: Heritage & Ancient Monument Extension

## Objective
Provide a specialized structural assessment layer for ancient monuments, religious shrines, fortresses, and vernacular historic structures in the Kutch/Bhuj earthquake zone.

## Key Design Principles
1. **Isolation from Ordinary Dataset**: Heritage assets (e.g., carved sandstone temples, lime-rubble bastions, traditional Bhungas) possess distinct structural mechanisms that cannot be evaluated using modern RC frame RVS tables. This module remains completely modular to prevent contaminating the core statistical datasets.
2. **Authoritative Conservation Standards**: Aligned with IS 13935:2009 (Heritage Strengthening), UNESCO/ICOMOS Guidelines for Cultural Heritage at Risk, and Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) restoration criteria.
3. **Preservation-First Guidelines**: Prioritizes reversible, breathable historic interventions (lime grouting, tie-rods, dismantling incompatible rigid concrete additions) over destructive cement alterations.
