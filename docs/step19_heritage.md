# Step 19: Heritage & Ancient Monument Extension

## Objective
Provide a dedicated structural and preservation assessment framework for ancient monuments, temples, forts, and vernacular historic assets (e.g. traditional Kutch Bhungas).

## Key Features
- **Dataset Isolation**: Evaluated independently from ordinary modern RC/brick masonry datasets to prevent statistical contamination.
- **Historic Structural Parameters**: Masonry bond (ashlar fine, coursed rubble in lime, multi-leaf stone), roof/vault systems (stone corbelled domes, true arches, timber framing), and historic decay (mortar leaching, timber rot, incompatible modern RCC interventions).
- **Standards**: Aligned with IS 13935:2009, ICOMOS, and Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) conservation practices.
